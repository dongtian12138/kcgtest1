## Release and use basis

Page 1 of each PDF states: "DISTRIBUTION STATEMENT A. Approved for public
release; distribution is unlimited." Section 105 of U.S. Copyright Act Title
17 states that U.S. copyright protection is not available for a work of the
United States Government:
<https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html#105>.

The previous official host, `landandmaritimeapps.dla.mil`, displayed a DLA
notice that the portal moved to `weaponssupportapps.dla.mil` on 8 August 2026.
The files above were retrieved from the new official host. On this workstation,
the local command-line CA bundle could not validate the DLA site's certificate
chain, so retrieval used the exact HTTPS URLs with certificate verification
disabled. File type, page count, document identity, amendment, date, and public
release statement were checked locally after download and cross-checked against
the DLA specification index.

Before any procurement or manufacturing decision, consult the current DLA
index, the controlling MIL-DTL-38999 general specification, and the applicable
qualification listing. Do not infer permission for any manufacturer CAD from
these public government specification sheets.
